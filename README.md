# Voice & Audio Experiments

I'm **Patrycja Krysik**, an audio engineer and sound designer with 20+ years of experience in recording, editing, post-production and sound design.

This repository is a small public notebook for experiments at the intersection of:

- voice and speech
- sound design
- psychoacoustics and auditory perception
- phonetics and diction
- AI-assisted audio workflows
- narrative audio and character voice design

## What I listen for

In voice work, I pay attention not only to technical quality, but also to:

- timbre and resonance
- articulation and intelligibility
- breath, phrasing and pacing
- performance continuity
- emotional intention
- perceptual clarity
- whether the voice actually fits the character and context

## Current areas of interest

I'm especially interested in how AI voice systems can preserve or distort:

- naturalness
- character identity
- emotional consistency
- timing and prosody
- perceived meaning

I also explore how source recording quality, performance style and acoustic consistency affect downstream voice generation and cloning.

## Narrative audio R&D

One ongoing area of experimentation is **Eliot Nomos / Limen**, a narrative project in which voice is treated as part of character architecture rather than as a neutral delivery mechanism.

The goal is not simply to find a voice that sounds impressive, but one that is dramaturgically correct: the right balance of intelligence, distance, presence, fragility, precision and emotional behavior.

That process involves critical listening, rejection of technically convincing but narratively wrong voices, and iterative comparison of alternatives.
