# Listening notes

This folder will contain short, practical observations from voice and audio experiments.

Topics may include:

- timbre and perceived identity
- intelligibility
- speech rhythm and prosody
- source-recording consistency
- emotional continuity
- artifacts and unnatural phrasing
- comparison of voice-generation approaches
- narrative fit vs. technical quality

The aim is not to document software engineering work. It is to document **critical listening and audio decision-making**.
